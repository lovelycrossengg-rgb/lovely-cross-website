FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services

Do you provide free quotes?
Yes! We provide completely free site inspections and detailed quotations for all projects. Just call or WhatsApp us to schedule a visit.
What areas do you serve?
We primarily serve Thoothukudi and surrounding areas in Tamil Nadu. For projects outside this region, please contact us to discuss feasibility.
Do you offer 24/7 emergency services?
Absolutely! We offer round-the-clock emergency services for electrical and plumbing issues. Call us anytime — day or night — for urgent repairs.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash, bank transfers, and all digital payments including UPI, Google Pay, and PhonePe. Payment terms can be discussed based on project scope.
Is your work guaranteed?
Yes! We guarantee quality workmanship on all our projects and stand behind our work with professional warranties. Customer satisfaction is our top priority.
